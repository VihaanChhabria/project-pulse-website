Community Milestone October 5, 2024

Over 100 Screenings Completed in Northside

edit_note

By Project Pulse Editorial

Last Saturday, the Northside community center buzzed with a different kind of energy. Amidst the usual weekend rhythms, our student-led team hit a landmark achievement, providing vital health diagnostics to our neighbors who need them most.

Last Saturday, Project Pulse reached a major milestone: our 100th community health screening. At the Northside Community Center, we transformed a simple morning into a life-changing event for dozens of our neighbors.

Key Outcomes

  • check_circle

    42 individuals received comprehensive health diagnostics in a single day.

  • check_circle

    Identified three critical cases of asymptomatic hypertension.

  • check_circle

    Connected high-risk patients directly with local primary care follow-ups.

By bringing healthcare directly to the neighborhood, we're not just collecting data—we're saving lives through connection and education.
